Problems we want to solve

Everyday shopkeeper problems. One organised system.

A practical breakdown of common problems and the matching website solution.

1. Customers don't know what your shop sells

People nearby may call repeatedly or visit without knowing product availability.

Website solution: Public online product catalogue and shop page.

2. Product prices and stock are hard to keep updated

Paper lists and scattered messages become outdated quickly.

Website solution: Shopkeeper can manage product details, prices, quantity and availability.

3. Customer orders get mixed up

Orders arriving through calls and messages can be missed or forgotten.

Website solution: Central incoming order dashboard and order status updates.

4. Customers keep asking for delivery updates

Shop staff lose time answering repeated status questions.

Website solution: Order status tracking for customers.

5. COD collection is difficult to track

It can be unclear whether an order is still pending, delivered or collected.

Website solution: COD order and collection status workflow.

6. Shop work is difficult to manage on a phone

Small screens and complicated software discourage daily use.

Website solution: Mobile-friendly shopkeeper workspace.

7. Shopkeepers need more ways to bring customers back

One-time buyers may forget to order again.

Planned: Repeat-order shortcuts, customer reminders and offers.

8. Marketing the shop takes extra effort

Sharing product details and shop links one by one takes time.

Planned: Shop QR code and WhatsApp sharing tools.

9. Sales and stock trends are hard to understand

Without clear reports, it is harder to spot best sellers and slow-moving items.

Planned: Sales analytics, stock alerts and growth insights.
