1. Sign in safely

  1. Open the Super Admin website and enter the Owner account email and password.
  2. Press Sign in securely.
  3. When asked, open the authenticator app already enrolled for this Owner account and enter its current 6-digit code.
  4. Press Verify code and open Owner dashboard. The code changes about every 30 seconds.
Never share your password, authenticator QR code, setup key, recovery details or 6-digit code. Do not approve login requests you did not start. A normal password reset does not bypass MFA.

If the code fails, check the phone's automatic date/time and use the same authenticator account. If you lost access, use the displayed recovery guidance and contact the project administrator for identity-verified recovery. Do not repeatedly restart or delete MFA factors.

2. Owner Command Center & overview

After successful Owner and MFA checks, the dashboard loads. The Command Center is the shortcut area; the overview cards show counts such as customers, shopkeepers, shops, products, orders, verified payments and subscriptions.

Refresh: reloads the summary and the main shopkeeper/business data shown by its handler. Use the section-specific Refresh button when you need one list updated.

Tap a Command Center tile to jump to that section. A count is a summary, not a button to edit the underlying records.

3. Business approvals, status & category controls

Shop approval & status

  1. Open Shop approval & status.
  2. Choose a business type in Filter by business type, or leave it on All business types.
  3. Review the shop card and its current status carefully.
  4. Use only the action displayed on that shop card. Confirm the action if a confirmation prompt appears.
  5. Press Refresh shops to reload the list.

Category-wise Business Control

Use Search all businesses / stores to find by shop name, city, phone, category or status. Category buttons narrow the displayed businesses. Refresh all reloads the business list. The platform has 11 business types: Grocery, Daily Needs, Garments, Jewellery, Restaurant, Hotel, Office / Company, Electronics, Medical, Digital Services and Other.

Approving, activating, deactivating or suspending a shop affects real access. Verify the shop identity and reason before confirming.

4. Shopkeeper & customer directories

Shopkeeper Contact Directory & Management

  1. Open the directory and type a name, mobile, shop name, city or address in its search field.
  2. Review the matching shopkeeper and linked shop details.
  3. Use Call or WhatsApp links only when you have a legitimate business reason. Use map/location links only to help identify the shop.
  4. Use any account control shown on the card only after checking the account and confirming the action.
  5. Press Refresh directory to reload.

The directory can show only information stored in the account/shop records. Missing address, phone or GPS data will not appear by magic.

Customer directory

Search by customer name or account status. This is a privacy-limited, read-only lookup; it does not expose private contact details or let you edit customer accounts. Press Refresh customers to reload.

5. Global orders, products & catalog

Global order console

Search the loaded order list by order number, shop, customer or status. Press Refresh orders to reload. This console is read-only; it does not change order status or fulfilment.

Global product console

Search loaded product/stock records using the search field and press its refresh button to reload. It is a cross-business read-only overview; it does not edit, delete or bulk-change shopkeeper products or stock.

Catalog management

  1. To add a product category, enter its name in the new category field and press Add category.
  2. To add a subcategory, choose its parent category, enter the subcategory name and press Add subcategory.
  3. Review the result and use Refresh catalog to reload.

Use clear names and avoid duplicate categories. Do not create a category just to test on a live store.

6. Payments & subscriptions

The payment section lists payment records and their gateway/status details. A successful status alone is not proof that money was verified.

  1. Find the payment record and inspect its linked order or subscription reference, amount, gateway and status.
  2. Only when the record is marked successful but not verified, and you have checked the linked record, use Verify & apply payment if shown.
  3. Confirm the prompt. The server-side verification action applies the payment and refreshes the related data.
  4. Press Refresh payments when needed.
Never manually claim a payment is received, edit payment records in the database, or ask a customer for their UPI PIN/OTP. Do not use this button for pending, failed or unknown transactions.

7. Security & audit activity

Open Security & audit activity to inspect recorded admin/security events. The page shows recent recorded events (up to the latest 100 returned by the current query), including action, entity, time and before/after data where available.

  • Refresh audit logs: reload the event list.
  • Live audit alerts: when the live connection is available, new recorded events refresh the list and show an alert.
  • Use audit records to investigate unusual changes. A missing event does not prove that nothing happened; only audited actions appear.

8. All-in-One Control Search

  1. After the dashboard is open, tap All-in-One Control at the top, or press Ctrl + K on a keyboard.
  2. Choose a category filter: All, Businesses, Shopkeepers, Customers, Orders, Products/stock, Payments/subscriptions, Security/audit or Pages/tools/settings.
  3. Type a short word such as a shop name, phone, order number, payment or “catalog”.
  4. Tap a result to jump to the matching section or loaded record. Press Escape or the close button to exit.
Search currently indexes visible controls and records already loaded into the dashboard. It is not a search of every historical database row or every private field.

9. Safe operating checklist

  • Use a trusted, updated device and keep its screen lock enabled.
  • Complete MFA every time it is requested. Sign out on a shared device.
  • Refresh and verify the exact shop/customer/order/payment before taking an action.
  • Do not share passwords, OTPs, QR codes, setup keys, service-role keys or payment secrets.
  • Do not disable security policies or bypass approval/payment checks to “fix” a screen.
  • Keep a note of the reason for important shop status or payment actions.
Remember: The Owner dashboard is for platform oversight. Shopkeepers should manage their own daily products, stock and customer operations in the Shopkeeper Portal.
